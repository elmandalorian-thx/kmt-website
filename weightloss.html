What is PCDC?

Phosphatidylcholine / Deoxycholate

PCDC contains deoxycholic acid, a naturally occurring molecule that disrupts fat cell membranes in localized areas, permanently destroying fat cells in targeted zones. This treatment is ideal for stubborn fat deposits that resist diet and exercise.
